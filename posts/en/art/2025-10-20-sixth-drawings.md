*Drawing inside text*

It's been a year. After such a long time, I actually felt like drawing again. I warmed up a bit and then just drew whatever came to mind. I think I had similar thoughts when I last posted drawings, but lately I've been trying to draw brighter pictures. Especially the last two pieces — I intentionally used bright, saturated colors, and even then I wasn't satisfied, so I went into Lightroom and adjusted the brightness and color with the same sensibility I use for photo editing.
