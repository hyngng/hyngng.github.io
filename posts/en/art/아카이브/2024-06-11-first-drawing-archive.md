These are drawings or doodles I made in elementary school. The notebook was too small to scan, so I archived them as photos instead. These were drawn in my very first sketchbook, which I got on May 24, 2014, and from then on, drawing has faithfully continued as a hobby up to the present.

There's actually much more than this, but after filtering down to only the presentable ones 🥲 this is what's left. Anyway, I think drawings only have meaning when someone looks at them, so aside from these, I plan to gradually post many other things I have.
