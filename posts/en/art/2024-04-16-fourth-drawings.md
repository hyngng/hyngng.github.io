I switched brushes and the feel is so different! I really love this rough texture, like drawing on paper, and it makes drawing so much more enjoyable 🥰 I feel like I've been missing out all this time.

I usually try to organize and post my drawings once I've accumulated enough, but once I get pulled into other things, the drawing habit I've built up easily falls apart. When I pick up the pen after a long time, my hand moves slowly too. Even though it's nothing impressive, it feels like the days I actually draw come irregularly, the cycle is inconsistent. After all, the inspiration has to strike first.
