*Everyday casual*

As always with drawing, most of the time goes into deciding what to draw rather than actually drawing. I make a habit of jotting down ideas whenever they come to mind, but when I look back at them later, the desire to draw has already faded, so I often end up just wasting time wondering what to draw. Still, occasionally when I do manage to turn an idea into an actual drawing, I feel like I get to know my own tastes and ideals a little better, so I find myself picking up the pen now and then.
